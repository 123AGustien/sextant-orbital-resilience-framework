# 🛰️ Fork Guide — Sextant Orbital Resilience Framework

## Intended Use for Forks

- research extensions
- scenario expansion
- alternative cascade models
- academic experimentation

---

## ⚠️ Required Rule for Forks

All forks must preserve:

- scenarios/ (input layer)
- core/ (simulation engine)
- api/ (execution layer)
- governance/ (rules layer)

---

## 🚫 Not Allowed in Forks

- collapsing architecture layers
- introducing operational control systems
- modifying deterministic simulation principles

---

## 🧠 Design Intent

This system is designed to evolve through modular extension, not structural fusion.
