1. Purpose

The Sextant Protocol™ Orbital Resilience Framework is a deterministic research and simulation environment designed to investigate how layered resilience mechanisms could support the recognition, verification, stabilisation and structured management of abnormal orbital-system conditions.

The framework brings together:

- scenario assessment;
- failsafe state-transition research;
- recovery-pathway simulation;
- independent validation;
- memory continuity;
- auditability;
- cascade-resilience research; and
- human / mission decision authority.

The objective is not to claim operational spacecraft capability.

The objective is to establish a transparent and repeatable environment in which the architecture can be tested, challenged, measured and independently reviewed.

---

2. Research Proposition

The principal research proposition is:

«A layered deterministic resilience architecture combining independent assessment, controlled state transitions, validation, memory continuity, auditability and human decision authority may improve recognition and structured management of developing abnormal conditions in a controlled orbital simulation environment.»

This remains a hypothesis.

Negative results are valid research outcomes.

6. Cascade Resilience

A central research question is whether developing abnormal conditions can be identified before they propagate into a more severe simulated state.

A conceptual cascade is:

INITIAL ANOMALY

↓

SYSTEM STRESS

↓

SECONDARY EFFECT

↓

REDUCED SYSTEM MARGIN

↓

ADDITIONAL ANOMALY

↓

CASCADE RISK

↓

STABILISATION / RECOVERY ASSESSMENT

The research does not claim guaranteed cascade prevention.

It investigates whether structured assessment and state management can provide earlier and more traceable decision-support.

10. Operational Boundary

The current framework is a research and simulation environment.

It is not:

- spacecraft control software;
- a spacecraft autopilot;
- a certified guidance system;
- a certified navigation system;
- a propulsion controller;
- an attitude-control controller;
- an autonomous collision-avoidance system;
- or a replacement for mission control.

The current implementation does not issue live spacecraft commands.

SIMULATED ACTION ≠ OPERATIONAL ACTION
