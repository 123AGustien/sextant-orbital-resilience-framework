---

Research Classification

This document describes a research and simulation framework.

It does not constitute:

- spacecraft flight software;
- operational mission-control software;
- certified guidance, navigation or control software;
- a safety case;
- a regulatory approval;
- a certification;
- or an operational command authority.

The framework is intended to provide a structured environment for technical investigation, simulation, validation, experimentation and independent review.

---

Evidence Position

The research propositions presented by Sextant Protocol™ remain hypotheses subject to testing, measurement, challenge and independent verification.

Successful simulation results shall not be interpreted as evidence of operational safety, flight readiness or mission suitability without appropriate engineering assurance and independent review.

The project therefore distinguishes between:

RESEARCH IMPLEMENTATION

and:

VALIDATED ENGINEERING CAPABILITY

and:

OPERATIONAL ASSURANCE

These are separate evidence levels.

---

Repository Transparency

The associated repository contains research source material, simulation components, scenario definitions, validation mechanisms, architecture documentation and audit artefacts intended to support technical examination.

Where practical, research artefacts should remain:

- version controlled;
- traceable;
- reproducible;
- independently examinable;
- and accompanied by documented assumptions and limitations.

Repository implementation should not itself be interpreted as certification evidence.

---

Review Principle

The project does not seek automatic acceptance of its claims.

It seeks evidence.

Independent reviewers are encouraged to challenge:

- architectural assumptions;
- mathematical models;
- resilience calculations;
- state-transition logic;
- validation methodology;
- scenario design;
- cascade assumptions;
- cybersecurity assumptions;
- human-factors assumptions;
- software implementation;
- experimental results;
- and interpretation of evidence.

A finding that contradicts the research proposition is considered a legitimate research result.

---

Core Research Sequence

The governing research sequence is:

BUILD → TEST → CHALLENGE → MEASURE → REPEAT → INDEPENDENTLY REVIEW

This sequence is intended to prevent architectural intention from being mistaken for demonstrated capability.

---

Operational Boundary

The Sextant Protocol™ Orbital Resilience Research Framework does not claim authority to control a real spacecraft.

The current research architecture is designed around:

OBSERVE → VERIFY → ASSESS → DECIDE → ACT → UPDATE

with the operational interpretation constrained by the human-authority boundary.

Within the current orbital research implementation:

AI / SOFTWARE ASSESSMENT ≠ HUMAN / MISSION AUTHORISATION

and:

SIMULATED ACTION ≠ OPERATIONAL COMMAND

No real spacecraft command pathway is claimed.

---

Human Authority

Human decision authority remains the final authority within the current research architecture.

The system may provide:

- anomaly assessment;
- verification;
- resilience analysis;
- recovery recommendations;
- warnings;
- escalation recommendations;
- alternative pathways;
- and decision-support information.

The human or mission authority determines the appropriate simulated decision where human authorisation is required.

The governing principle is:

NO RECOVERY ACTION EXECUTED UNTIL HUMAN AUTHORISATION

where such authorisation forms part of the simulation.

---

Simulation Boundary

The framework is intended to investigate simulated behaviour under controlled conditions.

Simulation scenarios may include:

- SIGNAL_LOSS;
- ORBITAL_DRIFT;
- TELEMETRY_CORRUPTION;
- POWER_FAILURE;
- INERTIAL_DESYNCHRONIZATION;
- concurrent abnormalities;
- cascade conditions;
- controlled fault injection;
- information-integrity disturbances;
- and human decision-support scenarios.

The presence of a scenario within the repository does not imply that the simulated relationships reproduce the behaviour of an actual spacecraft.

---

Research Integrity

The project recognises that simulation results depend upon:

- model fidelity;
- assumptions;
- input data;
- software implementation;
- configuration;
- thresholds;
- scenario design;
- experimental methodology;
- and validation quality.

Accordingly:

SIMULATION RESULT ≠ OPERATIONAL SAFETY EVIDENCE

and:

DETERMINISTIC ≠ CERTIFIED

and:

DETERMINISTIC ≠ SECURE

and:

AUTONOMOUS ASSESSMENT ≠ AUTONOMOUS AUTHORITY

---

Limitations

The present research framework may contain limitations including:

- simplified orbital models;
- simulated rather than operational data;
- limited physical-system fidelity;
- absence of real spacecraft interfaces;
- absence of certified flight hardware;
- prototype software characteristics;
- limited human-factors testing;
- limited statistical sample sizes;
- incomplete cybersecurity assurance;
- and absence of operational mission authority.

These limitations do not invalidate the research.

They define the boundaries within which its results should be interpreted.

Open Technical Review

The Sextant Protocol™ Orbital Resilience Research White Paper is intentionally presented as an open research proposition.

The project welcomes appropriate independent technical review.

The objective of review is not confirmation for its own sake.

The objective is to determine:

WHERE THE ARCHITECTURE WORKS

WHERE IT FAILS

WHY IT BEHAVES AS OBSERVED

WHAT REMAINS UNVALIDATED

This proposition remains open to challenge.

The project therefore adopts:

EVIDENCE BEFORE CLAIM

VALIDATION BEFORE ASSUMPTION

TRACEABILITY BEFORE ASSERTION

HUMAN AUTHORITY BEFORE AUTOMATION

SIMULATION BEFORE OPERATION
