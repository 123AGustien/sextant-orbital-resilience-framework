- system architecture;
- assumptions;
- scenario models;
- resilience calculations;
- state-transition logic;
- validation methodology;
- cascade behaviour;
- simulation fidelity;
- software implementation;
- cybersecurity;
- human factors;
- auditability;
- and research conclusions.

No reviewer, organisation, regulator, classification society, space agency or other institution should be considered to have endorsed or certified the framework unless such endorsement is separately documented.

---

Research Integrity

The project accepts the possibility that testing may demonstrate:

- no measurable improvement;
- unexpected system behaviour;
- false-positive conditions;
- false-negative conditions;
- unstable state transitions;
- inadequate simulation fidelity;
- human-factors limitations;
- or other deficiencies.

Negative results are legitimate research outcomes.

The objective is evidence, not confirmation of a predetermined conclusion.
