🛰️ Trial Manoeuvre Simulation Engine

The Trial Manoeuvre Engine is a deterministic simulation and assessment module within the Sextant Orbital Resilience Framework.

It receives scenario results from OrbitalEngineV1, selects the appropriate manoeuvre profile, simulates a recovery or stability pathway, and provides validated assessment data to the decision pipeline.

  • Scenario-based manoeuvre profile selection
  • Recovery pathway simulation
  • Stability verification
  • Golden Rule Engine authority integration
  • Audit trace generation

Operational Boundary:

The Trial Manoeuvre Engine is simulation-only. It does not generate real spacecraft control commands, autonomous navigation commands, collision avoidance actions, or CPA/TCPA alteration recommendations.

Future versions may incorporate validated navigation and trajectory simulation modules subject to mission rules, independent validation, and human operational authority.
